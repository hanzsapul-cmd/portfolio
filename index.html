About Me

I am Hanz Aaron G. Sapul, an aspiring Computer Engineer currently pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Computer Engineering at Holy Cross of Davao College. I am a curious, observant, and hands-on individual with a strong interest in technology, innovation, and problem-solving.

My interests include embedded systems, programming, electronics, robotics, automation, and computer vision. Through academic projects and hands-on experimentation, I have gained experience exploring microcontrollers, sensors, circuit integration, robotic mechanisms, and hardware-software development.

I enjoy turning ideas into practical solutions, learning new technologies, and continuously improving my technical abilities. I believe that every project is an opportunity to develop new skills, overcome challenges, and create something meaningful.

My goal is to become a skilled and innovative engineer who uses technology to develop practical solutions and make a positive impact.

“It's either you win or you learn, one step at a time.”
